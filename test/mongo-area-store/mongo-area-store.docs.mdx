Storage and five queries. Who may see an area, who may change one, and what a
legal area is all live elsewhere — which is why all three are tested without a
database and this class has no opinion about any of them.

## Wiring it up

```ts
const areas = MongoAreaStore.usingDefaultConnection();
```

## What is in the collection

A cap — a centre and a radius, in degrees and kilometres, as the entity holds
it. Not GeoJSON and no `2dsphere`: nothing here queries by geography.
Containment is `WatchArea.contains`, run in the process against a feed that is
already in memory, so a geospatial index would be a capability paid for at the
point it is least useful.

`(ownerId, createdAt)` and `visibility` are indexed, because the one read this
collection serves is "mine, plus everything shared".

## Reading rows back

`toPlainArea` fails closed on the one field that decides who may read an area:
an unrecognised `visibility` reads as `private`, never as shared. A typo in a
database must not publish somebody's home.
