The Tooltip component provides contextual information on hover or focus, styled according to the Nebula theme.

## Basic Tooltip

A simple tooltip that appears on top of the trigger element by default.
It inherits styling from the Nebula theme.

```jsx
<Tooltip content="Hello from Nebula! This is a basic tooltip.">
  <button type="button">Hover or Focus Me</button>
</Tooltip>
```
## Tooltip Positions
Demonstrates tooltips in different positions relative to their trigger element: top, bottom, left, and right.
The position is controlled by the `position` prop.
```jsx
<div style={{ display: 'flex', gap: '20px' }}>
  <Tooltip content="Tooltip on the Top" position="top">
    <span>Top Tooltip</span>
  </Tooltip>
  <Tooltip content="Tooltip on the Bottom" position="bottom">
    <span>Bottom Tooltip</span>
  </Tooltip>
  <Tooltip content="Tooltip on the Left" position="left">
    <span>Left Tooltip</span>
  </Tooltip>
  <Tooltip content="Tooltip on the Right" position="right">
    <span>Right Tooltip</span>
  </Tooltip>
</div>
```
## Rich Content and Delayed Tooltip
A tooltip with custom HTML content, enter and leave delays.
```jsx
<Tooltip
  content={
    <div>
      <h3>Nebula Info Card</h3>
      <p>
        This tooltip demonstrates <strong>rich HTML content</strong>.
        

        It appears after <em>500ms</em> and hides after <em>300ms</em>.
      </p>
    </div>
  }
  position="bottom"
  enterDelay={500}
  leaveDelay={300}
>
  <button type="button">Hover for Rich Content (Delayed)</button>
</Tooltip>
